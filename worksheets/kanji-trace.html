Before you trace a character, say its component keywords aloud, then the mnemonic, then the keyword. Hand, mouth and eye together — three encoding channels on one pass. Stroke order is not decoration: it determines how the character comes out when you write it fast, and it is how a dictionary finds it. Top-to-bottom, left-to-right, horizontal before crossing vertical, enclosing box before its contents, bottom stroke of a box last.
